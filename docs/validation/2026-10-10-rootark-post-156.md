Whole restore is not an instantaneous atomic visibility switch across quarantine, filesystem, JSON/SQLite, and cloud. On local filesystems, the durable preimage/coordinator model rolls back a failed pre-commit restore and resumes recovery after restart. After the local restore commit boundary, external provider writes are persisted as idempotent retry work; provider failures keep affected objects fenced and preserve the local barrier until recovery succeeds. A portable transaction across Node-managed local filesystem/SQLite operations and independent S3/Google APIs is not available. Windows Node.js cannot portably flush directory entries, so sudden-power-loss durability there is weaker than on POSIX. External writers bypassing the app gate and live provider interoperability remain outside proven guarantees. See [the transaction boundary](../backup-restore-transaction-boundary.md).
