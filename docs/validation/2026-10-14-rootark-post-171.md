## Security and acceptance boundaries

The selected seven-day chunk-session expiry resolves the retention-period decision recorded in the previous snapshot. It does not resolve issue #94 or PR #95: process-local upload limits do not establish deployment-wide enforcement.

PR #171 adds quarantine rollback recovery. Restore remains locally recoverable and is not globally atomic across quarantine, filesystem, JSON/SQLite, and cloud APIs. External writers and provider operations outside local transactions remain outside that recovery boundary. Do not infer portable power-loss atomicity from the merged change.

Issues #63–#68 remain incomplete: protected search, native Android, a complete Zero-Knowledge runtime, selective sync/Files On-Demand, destructive-change protection, and operation-scoped capability sharing are still open acceptance areas. Root.ark is not production-ready or an end-to-end Zero-Knowledge runtime.

Browser evidence in the [post-#169 record](2026-10-12-rootark-post-169.md) is tied to its stated earlier application source. No new browser acceptance is claimed for `bd9656c` in this snapshot.
